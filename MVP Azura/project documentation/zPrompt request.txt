==================================================
English trainer app
==================================================

This app is an interactive English vocabulary trainer designed for sentence-based learning:

Gap-Fill Exercises: Displays sentences with missing target words for users to type in.

Instant Feedback & Hints: Evaluates answers in real time, provides spelling hints, and highlights errors.

Audio & Pronunciation: Plays voice audio for target words to aid listening comprehension.

Progress Tracking: Tracks memory levels, correct/wrong counts, and session accuracy, saving progress locally.
